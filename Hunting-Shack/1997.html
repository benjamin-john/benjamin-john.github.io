Camp notes

Tom's first year hunting and he sits in the Pew Stand. Jeff has meetings in Denver that he can't miss. Flight gets in at Midnight and he drives up to the shack on one hour of sleep. He gets there just as everyone is going to the woods. He is grumpy and tired and later breaks hand when punching the camper. Anger management classes are scheduled but he doesn't attend. He spends the next several years trying to control his temper with minimal success.
