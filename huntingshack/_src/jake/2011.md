I had this 8 pointer coming in twice the night before at 20 and 30 yards. Both times I was surrounded by several does and didn't want to risk getting busted by drawing back. I had him on camera several nights in a row so I hoped he would come back again. Watching him walk away the second time without attempting a shot was the hardest thing I've ever done. It proved to be the right move as I made a solid shot at 30 yards the following evening, recovering him 120 yards later.
