Camp notes

First Weekend: Group included Tom, Jeff, Jake, Larry, Ben, Mike, Mitch, Cory, and Matt. Our first hunting season with a heated bathroom. Now we're livin'. It was a fun weekend and most people saw deer however they were all does. At the time of this update we cannot remember who saw what but I believe everyone saw at least one doe throughout the two weekends. This is the 2nd consecutive year with bucks only. It was a fun, comfortable weekend with very few shots heard in the woods. This year we were able to enjoy an additional TV in the main cabin.

Second Weekend: 2nd weekend participants were Jake, Jeff, Carson, Larry, Ben, Sam, Mike, Mitch and Cory. Well, not much better than the first weekend however on Saturday we were able to bag one deer. Mike was walking down the WMA trail that starts on the corner of our property. As he made his way down the trail he spotted a deer walk out into the opening. He took a shot and found hair but no blood. It took off down the trail and into the taller grass. It made its way nearly to the point where Mike usually hunts but then doubled back a ways and came into Mitch's area. After a couple shots Mitch was able to get it. It was a 4 point buch but had a pretty large body. We know because we had to drag it through the boggy grass and across the drainage ditch. That was it for shots fired. All in all a very fun 2 weekends.
