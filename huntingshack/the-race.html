House rules

  • Bucks are scored in antler points. Does are scored in does tagged.
  • Race totals start in 1997 (Jake's first year at camp). Entries marked * don't count toward the totals.
  • Button-buck rule: after 2006 we declared that points need to be actual (1" and polished).
  • Race totals don't include every deer harvested.
