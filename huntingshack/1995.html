Camp notes

Papa shoots a doe out of Jeff's stand and later takes Larry's stand. Larry goes 40 yards behind him and shoots 2 deer, a doe and a 3 point buck.. We actually left camp on Sunday morning because we filled all of our tags. Papa drags Larry's buck back to camp without complaining. This is the last year that Gary, Razor and Gordon camp near us. Larry and Jeff buy the pickup box camper from Steve.
